# Data corrections and governance

For the published V1.0.0 package, a correction request should identify the release version, affected public observation or event-group ID (if applicable), source title, public source evidence, and the proposed factual change.

**Contact currently in use:** [info@peakcountryhail.com](mailto:info@peakcountryhail.com). A dedicated `data@peakcountryhail.com` inbox has been proposed in the Source of Truth but has **not been confirmed active**. Do not imply that it is an available contact until configured and tested. GitHub Issues may be enabled at publication for source, methodology, accessibility and data errors only—not customer service, repair estimates, insurance claims or appointments.

Reports are reviewed under approved source-precedence and stable-ID rules. Do not silently change observation identity, data values, geographic classification, or evidence independence. Corrected releases require human review, changelog entry, updated version/checksums, archive continuity and explicit publication authorization. Public issue reports must not include private customer information, credentials, account data, restricted source files or personal records.

See `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, `METHODOLOGY.md`, and `LIMITATIONS.md`.
