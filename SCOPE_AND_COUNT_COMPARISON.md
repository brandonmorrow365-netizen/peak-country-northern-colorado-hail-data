# Why Peak Country's hail-history counts can differ

**Publisher:** [Peak Country Auto Hail Repair & Paintless Dent Repair](https://peakcountryhail.com/). **Version:** 1.0.0, 2016–2025 research data. **Status:** published October 11, 2026.

This research dataset and the existing Peak Country website's hail-history views answer **different questions**. A difference in totals is not necessarily a defect and must never be erased by quietly changing counts.

| Feature | This research dataset | Existing Peak Country website history | Live tracker / current alerts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Period | Calendar years **2016–2025**, frozen v1 | May show a rolling or selected period, including 2026 | Current conditions and preliminary events |
| Geographic inclusion | Entire **Weld and Larimer Counties** plus a separately derived **25-statute-mile** subset | Uses a **50-mile** historical display; confirm each page's particular center and filter in T19 | Varies by radar/alert/marker layer |
| 25-mile methodology | WGS84 geodesic from fixed Lincoln Park reference at 40.424896787198563, -104.69350052421541 | Not asserted to use the same reference point or county inclusion | Not directly comparable |
| Sources | Finalized NOAA/NCEI backbone **plus approved supplemental CoCoRaHS and source-lineage logic**; SPC preliminary derivatives; radar context separate | NOAA/NCEI-oriented historic presentation | NWS alerts, SPC reports and/or MRMS radar may be preliminary/contextual |
| Counting units | 1,844 confirmed observation lineages; Weld 773, Larimer 1,071; **1,064** within 25 miles; **1,119** conservative event groups | Display figures depend on its own source vintage, chosen radius and period | Not a final observation or unique-storm total |
| Comparison allowed? | Descriptive within the documented fixed methodology | Do not compare raw totals with the research dataset as though equivalent | Do not combine live radar hits with confirmed ground reports |

**The 25-mile subset overlaps the county master** and must not be added to the county totals. A county name or local community label is not a municipal-boundary claim. The Lincoln Park point is neither Peak Country's business address nor a source observation.

The website's locked intended canonical **human-facing research project** route is `https://peakcountryhail.com/northern-colorado-hail-history/`. That address currently presents a different 50-mile historical view; it is **not yet verified as a live landing page for this 25-mile/multi-source v1 dataset**. T19 must update the public explanation so the existing 50-mile product and the new research v1 are both clearly labeled. Do not silently replace the current website's 50-mile data or claim this research dataset was already indexed there.
