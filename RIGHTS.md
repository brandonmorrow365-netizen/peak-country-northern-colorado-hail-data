# Rights and source boundaries — version 1.0.0

The Peak Country Northern Colorado Hail History Dataset is a compilation of sourced observations, contextual data, geographic QA and Peak Country-original analysis. No claim of exclusive ownership of weather facts is made.

- **NOAA/NCEI:** canonical historical observation backbone. Underlying public-domain federal information is not licensed by Peak Country.
- **NWS:** preliminary/reference information; source specificity and provenance must remain visible.
- **SPC:** preliminary derivative material, not automatically independent corroboration of matching NWS local storm reports.
- **CoCoRaHS:** supplemental ground observations; independently licensed under CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/; official legal code: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/legalcode). Preserve appropriate attribution, license link, normalization/change notices and separate third-party rights.
- **mPING:** raw layer NOT_USED/excluded, not redistributed.
- **MRMS/MESH:** radar context only, not ground confirmation. Archive unavailable 2016–2019, partial from 2020-10-14, available archive 2021–2025. Coverage availability and positive counts must never be presented as uniform for the full decade.
- **Census TIGER/Line:** geographic/county quality assurance, not a hail observation source.

The fixed Lincoln Park, Greeley, Colorado reference at 40.424896787198563, -104.69350052421541 is independently chosen Peak Country methodology for WGS84 geodesic distance. It is not a business/customer address, city-membership flag, observation source, or evidence of business presence. No City of Greeley GIS data was used and no City GIS licensing or attribution obligation attaches.
