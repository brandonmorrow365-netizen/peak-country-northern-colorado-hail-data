# Data limitations and responsible interpretation

This version describes reported ground hail observations for Weld and Larimer Counties, Colorado, in 2016–2025. Neither report counts nor hail-day counts estimate the complete number of storms or demonstrate a trend in actual hail frequency. Reporting and observer coverage vary by source, place and year.

- **Observation, event and hail day are different units.** A stable observation ID represents a reporting lineage, not a unique storm. Event grouping is conservative; one hail day may include multiple reporting lineages.
- **No property-level inference.** A nearby report does not demonstrate hail at a particular location, actual vehicle damage, repair needs or insurance coverage.
- **Source independence.** SPC can republish NWS LSR information, and CoCoRaHS ground reports may be reused in other sources. Repeated publishing of one lineage does not increase independent corroboration.
- **Ground size uncertainty.** Source hail magnitudes are preserved without averaging disagreement. Radar-derived MESH context is not a ground measurement.
- **Radar coverage break.** MRMS archived context is unavailable 2016–2019, partial from October 14, 2020, and available but not guaranteed complete 2021–2025. Unavailable is **not** a measured zero. Comparisons of radar-positive counts across these periods require archive-coverage adjustment.
- **Geographic caveats.** Source locality labels are not municipal boundaries. The 25-mile Greeley reference is a geodesic research device, not a business address or proof of city membership. County and radius subsets overlap.
- **Known acquisition gaps.** A complete original historical NWS LSR archive could not be verified; SPC has a 2022-02-24 HTTP 502 file retrieval gap; media/photos from CoCoRaHS are excluded; raw mPING is excluded. Twenty-nine unresolved provisional observation identities are held outside the active count.
- **No new statistical analysis.** The descriptive figures and summaries are not a significance test, climate-change analysis, normalized regional exposure assessment or property-risk model.
- **Versioned source corrections.** Source agencies may revise their historical files. The approved retrieval versions and processing methods are recorded separately; changes after release require an approved versioned correction.
