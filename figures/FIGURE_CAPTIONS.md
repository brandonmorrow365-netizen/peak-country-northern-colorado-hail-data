## F2: Observations by calendar month

2016–2025 aggregated months; not climatic trend proof.

**Alt:** Bar chart of observations aggregated by calendar month over 2016–2025. May has 515 observations, June 453 and July 386, while November has none. Counts reflect the assembled reports, not a climate trend or uniform reporting coverage.
